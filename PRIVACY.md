# Privacy policy

Biblioteques WiFi Auto-Login (unofficial) does not collect, transmit, or share
any data with the developer or any third party.

- Your carnet/DNI and PIN are stored locally in your browser via
  `browser.storage.local`, in plain text (browser extension storage is not
  encrypted). Anyone with access to your Firefox profile can read them.
- They are only ever sent to the library portal (`xsf-biblioteques.diba.cat`)
  when the extension submits the login form, exactly as if you typed them in.
- No analytics, telemetry, or remote code.

Uninstalling the extension deletes the stored credentials.
